About Us

Saffron & Sage was born from a simple idea: that great food brings people together. Since 2012 we have been serving seasonal, locally sourced dishes in a warm and welcoming space.

Our chefs blend traditional techniques with modern creativity, while our team ensures every guest feels at home. Whether it is a quiet dinner for two or a lively family celebration, we are here to make it memorable.
